## Communities

- [Ichi](https://ichi.city/) - A small-web home for making a personal presence online.
- [Midnight Pub](https://midnight.pub/) - A virtual pub for writing and conversation.
- [Special Fish](https://special.fish/garden) - Personal logs, links, and quiet exploration through an unusual social space.
- [Are.na](https://www.are.na/) - Collect and connect ideas through shared channels.
- [Halfbakery](https://www.halfbakery.com/) - A community devoted to inventing, proposing, and debating unusual ideas.
- [Interactive Fiction Community Forum](https://intfiction.org/) - A gathering place for makers and players of interactive stories.
- [HTML Zine Club](https://htmlzine.club/index.html) - A meeting point for handmade HTML publishing and zines.
- [Status Cafe](https://status.cafe/) - A small social space built around brief status updates.
- [HTML Journal](https://journal.miso.town/) - A journal-oriented corner of the small web.
- [imood](https://www.imood.com/) - A social site organized around sharing how you feel.
- [MelonLand](https://melonland.net/) - A creative web community centered on personal sites and expressive internet culture.
- [IndieWeb](https://indieweb.org/) - A community and knowledge base for owning your presence on the web.
- [Nekoweb](https://nekoweb.org/) - A home for personal websites and the people making them.
- [32-Bit Cafe](https://discourse.32bit.cafe/) - A community for people making a more personal web.
- [Oozing Goo](https://oozinggoo.ning.com/) - A remarkably specific community for lava-lamp enthusiasts.
- [Neocities](https://neocities.org/) - Personal website hosting with a community of independent web makers.
- [Human Brain Cloud](https://www.humanbraincloud.com/) - A massively multiplayer word-association experiment.
- [Decro](https://www.decro.net/) - Explore creative groups on a zoomable map and share work in their rooms.
- [HTML Energy](https://html.energy/) - A community celebrating hand-written HTML through HTML Day meetups, freewrites, and a podcast.
- [Values Based Software](https://valuesbased.software/) - A gathering and talk series about software as a form of self-expression.
- [The Yesterweb](https://yesterweb.org/) - An archive of a web revival community, including its manifesto, zine, and account of how the community organized.
- [threads.garden](https://threads.garden/) - Start a conversation with a link and build a thread out of replies from around the web.
